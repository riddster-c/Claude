FAQ

Questions we get before the first call.

Do I need a huge stash of miles already?

Not at all. A lot of our best work happens with people who have zero miles today — we build a 12-to-18-month runway using the cards and spend patterns you already have.

What does it actually cost?

Single-trip strategy starts at $149. Ongoing coaching is month-to-month. Award-booking concierge is a flat fee per ticket, quoted upfront — never a percentage of what the ticket "would have cost".

Will you book the flights for me?

Only if you want us to. Our concierge tier handles the search and ticketing end-to-end. Our strategy and coaching tiers give you the playbook so you can pull the trigger yourself.

Which programs do you cover?

All three global alliances, the major transferable-points ecosystems (Amex, Chase, Capital One, Bilt, Citi), plus Marriott Bonvoy, World of Hyatt, IHG One Rewards, and Hilton Honors.

Are you affiliated with any airline?

Nope. We're independent. We don't take airline commissions and we don't run credit-card affiliate links — so the advice you get is the advice that's best for you.
