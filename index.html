About

Who's whispering?

I'm a Singapore-based points obsessive who books the trips other people assume are out of reach. Business class to southern Africa, remote corners of Oman, ryokans in Hokkaido — flown on miles, booked through routes and programs most people don't know exist.

I don't sell credit cards. I don't earn a cut when you sign up for anything. I just think the redemption side of this hobby is criminally under-covered in Asia — so I'm covering it.
